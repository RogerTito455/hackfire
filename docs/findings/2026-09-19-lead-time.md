# The lead time for La Atalaya: 6 h 8 min, and how it was computed

**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Area:** pitch figure, slice 4 (#5)

## The number

**6 h 8 min** (368 minutes), from the forecast issued at 13:30 UTC (15:30 local) on 23 July 2026 to the first satellite hotspot within 3 km of La Atalaya, an MTG detection at 19:38:34 UTC (21:38 local), 2.42 km from the estate's outline, medium confidence.

**For the pitch, in two sentences:**

> With only the satellite hotspots available at 15:30 local time on 23 July 2026, HackFire flagged La Atalaya as in the fire's predicted path; the first hotspot within 3 km of the estate came at 21:38, a lead time of 6 hours 8 minutes. The number comes from satellite data only and says nothing about when the authorities warned anyone.

## How it is computed

`pnpm data:lead-time` (no network, no key) runs `backend/app/lead_time.py` over the cached files and writes `data/lead_time_la-atalaya.json`, which `GET /api/lead-time` serves and the dashboard shows once the slider passes the flag.

- **Flagged:** the first forecast in `data/spread_2026-07-23.geojson` whose predicted spread, within 6 hours, touches the estate's OSM outline. A forecast only uses hotspots observed up to its own issue time (`backend/tests/test_spread.py` checks this), so the flag never sees the future.
- **Reached:** the first hotspot, in observation time, within 3 km of that outline. The radius is a choice, made once: 3 km is the pixel size of MTG, the sensor behind 62% of the hotspots. Inside the outline never happens in the data, and the closest hotspot ever is 0.52 km, on 24 July at 22:15 UTC.

| Radius | First hotspot (UTC) | Sensor | Lead time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 km | 23 Jul 21:39 | Sentinel-3A | 8 h 9 min |
| **3 km** | **23 Jul 19:38** | **MTG** | **6 h 8 min** |
| 4 km | 23 Jul 19:28 | MTG | 5 h 58 min |
| 5 km | 23 Jul 18:28 | MTG | 4 h 58 min |
| 1 km | 24 Jul 22:15 | Sentinel-3B | not a lead time: a day later |

A bigger radius gives a shorter, safer number, and 3 km sits on the plateau between 2.5 and 3 km, so the figure does not move with small changes of the radius. Say "within 3 km", never "reached the estate".

## Caveats to say before the jury asks

- **It is in-sample.** The model's constants (1 km/h speed floor, 10% decay per half hour, 3-hour window) were chosen while looking at this same fire, after the first version un-flagged La Atalaya during a satellite gap ([the spread finding](2026-09-19-spread-cone-model.md)). Nobody has run the model on another fire. Extension 3 (Devin, #19) is where a held-out check would go.
- **The flag is a prediction.** At 13:30 UTC the model predicted the estate would be reached in 5 hours, that is at about 18:30 UTC; the first hotspot within 3 km came an hour later. That agrees, but it is one event.
- **One hotspot decides "reached".** It is a single MTG pixel of medium confidence. Six hotspots lie within 3 km in the first hour after it, and MODIS adds more within two hours, so the arrival does not rest on that detection alone; but the exact minute does.
- **No claim about the authorities.** We do not have the time of any official warning, so nothing compares the lead time with one.
