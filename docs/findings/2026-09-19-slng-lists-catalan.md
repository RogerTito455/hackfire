# SLNG's catalogue now lists Catalan

**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Area:** SLNG

## What happened

PLAN.md (section 4, "Out") and issue #7 say SLNG does not support Catalan. On 2026-09-19 SLNG's models-by-language page lists Catalan for:

- Fish TTS S2.1 Pro (text to speech, SLNG-hosted in the Netherlands, Germany, Australia, UK)
- Soniox TTS RT v1 (text to speech)
- Deepgram Nova 3 and Soniox Speech AI RT v5 (speech to text)

Not tested: nobody has synthesised or transcribed Catalan with them yet.

## What we do about it

Nothing for the build. The demo fire is in Ávila, the residents speak Spanish, and Catalan stays out of scope. It is worth knowing for the Q&A, because the jury is in Barcelona: "Catalan is in SLNG's catalogue; the agent's language is a configuration choice" is a fair answer, once someone has checked that the voice sounds right.
